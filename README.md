# Smoke & Sirens

A 3D driving game set in a stylised London, built around **Alex Philip's cybersecurity portfolio**.

Play it at **https://alexphilip01.github.io/MY-CITY/** once GitHub Pages is on.

## What's in it
- Six portfolio stops (About, Skills, Projects, Experience & education, Certifications, Contact) placed around London landmarks
- London with the Thames, bridges, roundabouts, traffic lights, zebra crossings, buses that pick up passengers, high streets and car parks
- Scenery from the original 3D drive: Buckingham Palace, Royal Holloway, SKCET, the SHIELD store, a zoo, hospital, fire station, school and playground
- Six towns and villages joined by three-lane motorways, with coach depots, cattle, farms, mountains, trains on viaducts and aeroplanes
- Map navigation: press **M**, tap a place, and follow the route
- Police chases, story jobs, Alex's grand saloon (press **G** to call it)

## Controls
WASD or arrows to drive and walk · E get in/out · Space handbrake · Shift sprint · scroll for cruise control · C camera · M map · G call your car · P pause

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128. A single `index.html`, no build step.
